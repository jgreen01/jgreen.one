# AI crawlers fall into three kinds, and the trade-off differs for each:
#
#   Training        GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended
#                   Blocking costs nothing in citations.
#   Search          OAI-SearchBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot
#                   Blocking removes this site from AI answers.
#   User-triggered  ChatGPT-User, Claude-User, Perplexity-User
#                   Blocking means someone who asked about this site gets nothing.
#
# This is a personal site that exists to be read, and the writing is given away
# rather than monetised, so there is nothing here that training crawlers take and
# nothing to be compensated for. Blocking any of the three would only make the
# site harder to find.
#
# Reviewed 2026-08-31. Revisit if the site ever hosts something worth licensing.
#
